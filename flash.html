ESP-SDR firmware installer

Flash via USB Serial/JTAG or a USB-to-UART adapter. For ESP-WebSDR, UART adapters must support 2 MBaud.

Before connecting

  1. Open this page in desktop Chrome, Edge, or Firefox.
  2. If connection fails, hold down the BOOT button on your board while connecting.
  3. On Linux, check udev rules / serial permissions if access is denied.

Installing replaces the current firmware and partition table.
